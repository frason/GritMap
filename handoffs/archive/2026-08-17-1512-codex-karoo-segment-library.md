## Outcome

transport-neutral, atomic JSON transfer-package importer. Coco Jumbo is installed and
visible as one segment on the connected Karoo 3. The transfer contract can carry immutable
segment geography, rider history, and a separately stored baseline pacing plan generated by
a phone/cloud/manual producer. Live deterministic matching remains authoritative.

## Hazards and blockers

- `connectedDebugAndroidTest` cleared the production package's Room data and app-specific
  external files during this session. Coco Jumbo was restored afterward. Do not run device
  instrumentation against the user's active Karoo installation without first backing up its
  app data or separating the instrumentation application/package.
- The transfer contract and inbox exist, but a wireless phone-to-Karoo transport is not yet
  implemented. Current delivery uses ADB/app-specific staged files.
- The on-device Needle/Cactus model remains fail-closed until an exact licensed Android
  runtime/model artifact, format, version, and SHA-256 are pinned. Deterministic provisional
  pacing remains the safe fallback.
- Local `main` is five Karoo commits ahead of `origin/main` at this handoff.
