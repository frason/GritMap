GritMap Karoo extension 0.10.46 (version code 76), screen preview beta. Karoo 3 only.

What changed since 0.10.45: redesigned live data screens for readability on the Karoo (segment progress, goal, ahead/behind, cardiac fields), same direct Bluetooth transfer from the iPhone app, same experimental flags (all off by default).

Needs: a Hammerhead Karoo 3 on software 1.538.2049 or newer, the GritMap iPhone app from TestFlight, and a power meter and heart-rate monitor on your bike.

Install over the existing GritMap extension. Do NOT uninstall it or clear its data. This version updates its on-device database; once it has run, going back to 0.10.45 is not supported. Full steps: docs/BETA_KAROO_INSTALL.md (Hammerhead Companion share-link method).

Advisory training displays only, not medical measurements. Report problems with your Karoo software version, this version and what you were doing. Do not post private emails or Apple IDs publicly. Support: help@opensegments.org
