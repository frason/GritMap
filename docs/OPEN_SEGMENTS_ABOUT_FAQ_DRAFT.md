# Open Segments About page FAQ (DRAFT for Jason's edit and lawyer review)

Plain-language draft. Not legal text: items marked **[LAWYER]** state licence, ownership or data-handling claims and must be reviewed with `docs/PRIVACY_CHANGES_FOR_REVIEW.md` before publishing. Facts come from the current Terms/Privacy pages on the Open Segments branch (ODbL 1.0, optional credit, 5 publications per account per UTC day, Link only, takedown by request). ## The basics

**What is Open Segments?**
A free, open catalog of cycling segments: a named stretch of road or trail with its route, distance and elevation. Anyone can browse, view and download segments without an account. It is run as an independent service, not as part of a ride-tracking company.

**What can I do with a segment I find here?**
Look at its map and elevation profile, see its distance, climb and grade by quarter-mile, and download it to use on your own bike computer or in apps that read segment files. Segments carry the creator's credit if they chose to give one. Check current road conditions and access yourself; listing a segment does not verify that it is safe or permitted to ride.

**How do I add my own segments?**
Sign in with your email (we send a one-time code), then upload a ride you recorded yourself (FIT or GPX file), pick the section you want, review the start and finish, and publish. Your full recording stays in your browser; only the selected route, its elevation, name and credit are shared. You can publish up to five segments per day. **[OPEN]** Publishing from the GritMap phone app is planned, not available yet.

**Should I make it listed or link-only?**
Listed segments appear in search, maps and lists for everyone. Link only segments stay out of discovery, but anyone with the link can open and share them. A link-only segment can be made public later; a public one cannot go back to link-only.

## Rights and data

**Do I own a segment I publish?** **[LAWYER]**
You keep whatever rights you have in your own ride recording, which stays on your device. When you publish a segment you offer that segment's data to everyone under the Open Database Licence (ODbL 1.0), with your credit if you chose one. Only publish routes from rides you recorded yourself and have the right to share. Do not copy segments from another platform in breach of its terms.

**What does the licence mean for segment data?** **[LAWYER]**
Segment data is open under ODbL 1.0: anyone may use, copy and build on it, including commercially, if they credit the source and share their changes to the data under the same terms. Open Segments does not promise that anyone can be stopped from reusing data that has already been published.

**Can I edit or delete a segment after publishing?** **[LAWYER]**
Published route content and credit cannot be edited. You can ask us to remove a segment (see Help: reports and removal requests), but copies people already downloaded cannot be recalled. Deleting your account detaches it from your segments; it does not remove their public credit or licensed data.

**Is there a way to get all the segments?** **[OPEN, LAWYER]**
We intend to publish bulk downloads of the open catalog, because the data is open. This is not available yet and the terms for it need review before we promise a format or schedule. Until then, individual segments can be downloaded one at a time.

**What does credit look like, and does it show my email?**
Credit is optional. If you choose "Credit me", the segment shows your account name (a generated name like rider-12345678, which you cannot yet change). Otherwise it shows Anonymous. Your sign-in email is never used as public credit.

## Privacy and safety

**What do you know about me?**
Browsing needs no account. If you sign in we keep your email to send codes and your account name. Your ride files are opened in your browser and are not uploaded in full. The Privacy page lists exactly what is sent and when.

**How do I report a problem or ask for removal?**
Use "Report a concern" on any segment, or the Help page. We may hide or remove segments over safety, privacy, rights or abuse concerns. Check your start and finish before publishing, especially near a home or other private place.

**Is Open Segments free? Who runs it?** **[OPEN]**
Browsing and downloading are free with no subscription. Who operates the service and how it is funded must be stated here once the legal structure and contact address are decided.

## GritMap

**What is GritMap?**
GritMap is a separate app for planning and riding with a bike computer: a phone app plus a Karoo app that shows live pacing against segments. It is one of the apps that can use Open Segments.

**How does GritMap work with Open Segments?**
In GritMap you can find an Open Segments segment, save it, and send it to your Karoo. Open Segments does not need GritMap, and GritMap does not own the catalog. Segments you view or save in the phone app are stored on your phone. **[OPEN]** Liked segments and publishing from the phone app depend on features not yet released.
